Who we are

Rooted in Kainuu, connected across borders.

Based in Finland's Kainuu Region and active since 2016, Launch Pad Finland ry connects people, ideas and organisations across borders — and puts them to work close to home.

We focus on internationalisation, regional and international networking, and the participation, well-being and quality of life of people in our region — especially young people and young adults.

What started as youth-focused work has grown into something broader: today we run activities and services for young adults, families and local communities alike. Throughout, we've built close partnerships with regional and municipal authorities, educational institutions, local businesses, associations and other stakeholders.

We believe international cooperation, diverse backgrounds and fresh ideas make communities stronger — more skilled, more connected, more resilient. That's the opportunity we create for people to learn, participate, grow their skills and become active members of their communities.

How we work

International

International cooperation with a local focus.

International cooperation sits at the heart of what we do. We bring global ideas, people and networks into Kainuu — and open doors for local people and organisations to connect with partners abroad.

Our projects have spanned youth participation, social entrepreneurship, intercultural communication, community development, creative learning and regional cooperation.
